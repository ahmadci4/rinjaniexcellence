Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to hike Mount Rinjani?
The best time to hike is during the dry season, from April to November. The national park is officially closed from January to March due to heavy rain and extreme weather for safety reasons.
Is it safe for beginners?
While Rinjani is a challenging volcano, beginners with good physical fitness can successfully reach the Crater Rim. However, the Summit Attack (3,726m) requires strong stamina and mental resilience. We always prioritize your pace and safety.
Do I need to carry my own tent and food?
No! Our packages are fully inclusive. Our dedicated porters will carry all camping equipment, sleeping bags, and food. You only need to carry a small daypack with your personal items, water, and camera.
How do I book and pay?
You can easily book by contacting us via WhatsApp. A small deposit is required to secure your e-Rinjani ticket, and the rest can be paid in cash upon your arrival in Lombok.
