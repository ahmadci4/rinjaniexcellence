MOUNT RINJANI TREKKING 2 DAYS 1 NIGHT SUMMIT

Mount Rinjani 2 days 1 night trek start from Sembalun to the Sembalun Crater rim, camping tent, climb to summit Rinjani and return to Sembalun village in East Lombok.

This Hiking Mount Rinjani program is designed for climbers or trekkers in a good level of fitness and able to walk up and down for an average of 8.5 hours per day.

(Sembalun Village - Sembalun Crater Rim - Summit)

Arrival Day On Lombok / Day 0:

We'll pick you up at the Lombok airport or other places on Lombok island and transfer to the hotel in Senaru or Sembalun village (on request). Meet your trekking guide who will provide guidance to better prepare you for the journey, and we will guide you to Sindang Gile waterfall for warming up before trekking Rinjani next day, back to hotel provided, then dinner.

Day 1:

Wake up at 06:30 and get breakfast. After that, at 07:00 we will drive you to the village of Sembalun (1100 m) and register at the Rinjani Information Center Office Sembalun. Trekking starts from the village of Sembalun around 8:00 am. We start with a gentle climb through open meadow. After about 2 hours of walking, we will reach Pos 1 (1300m), and another 40 minutes to Pos 2 (1500m). We will continue to Pos 3 (1800m) and have lunch here. After lunch and rest, we will go to Sembalun crater rim (2639m). It takes 3 hours including a prolonged steep climb. We will camp the night on the crater rim. We can enjoy the view of sunrise and sunset.

Day 2:

We wake up around 02:00 am under the cover of darkness with a hot drink and a light breakfast. We will start heading to the summit of Rinjani at 03:00 am. We start with a moderate trek, followed by a fairly easy but longer trek. Final approach that involves climbing up steep volcanic scree looser. At 06:00 am, we can reach the summit (3726 m) with a beautiful sunrise, and panoramic view of Lombok island, the island of Bali, the island of Sumbawa and Segara Anak Lake. After that, we will descend into the crater rim Sembalun within 2 hours and have breakfast here. Then will continue to go down to the village of Sembalun, which takes 5 hours to reach down. Car will take us to the village of Senaru (Rinjani Excellence) and we'll transfer to your next destination.

Mount Rinjani Trekking 3 Days 2 Night

Sembalun Crater Rim - Summit, Lake & Hot Spring - Senaru Crater Rim

Arrival Day On Lombok Islands

We provide private pick-up service across Lombok Airport / Central of lombok (Kuta Mandalika, Mataram, Senggigi, etc) or North/East harbors. Transfer to Senaru Village and Check in at the Senaru accommodation with hot/cold water, briefing and free at your leisure time. If your time allowing 3 hours before sunset you can visit the Famous Waterfalls in Senaru area (Sendang Gile and Tiu Kelep).

Day 01: The First Day Doing Hiking

Our guide and driver meet you at Senaru accommodation and will pick you up at lobby at 06.00 am. Then transfer to Sembalun Village by car. Register/check in barcode of the ticket and check up at Rinjani Information Center (RIC) (1,156 m). Guide provides briefing describing the route details. Walk through open grassland used as cattle grazing. Rest at Pos 1 (1,300m), Pos 2 (1,500m), and Pos 3 (1,800m). Ascend to Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m). Camp overnight and enjoy sunset views.

Day 02: Summit, Lake & Hot Spring

Wake up at 02:00 am, have a light breakfast. Begin the challenging 3-hour trek to the Summit (3,726m) for a spectacular sunrise. Return to Sembalun Crater Rim camp for a second breakfast. Proceed down to Segara Anak Lake (2,000m) which takes about 3 hours. Relax and swim at the Natural Hot Springs nearby. After lunch at the lake, leave the lake and climb up to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m). Camp the second night here and enjoy the magnificent sunset over Mount Agung in Bali.

Day 03: Descent to Senaru Village

After breakfast and enjoying the sunrise, we will descend down to Senaru village. The descent takes around 4-5 hours through thick tropical forest where you may spot the rare black monkey. We will arrive at Rinjani Excellence office in Senaru around 1:00 PM. From there, we will transfer you to your next destination in Lombok.

Mount Rinjani Trekking 4 Days 3 Night

(Sembalun Crater Rim - Summit Lake & Hot spring - Senaru Crater Rim)

Day 0:

Pick up service from any location in Lombok (Airport, Harbors, Mataram, Senggigi, etc) then transfer to Senaru Village and Check in at Senaru Accommodation. Briefing and free at your leisure. If time allowing (3 hours before sunset) you can visit the Famous Waterfalls in Lombok (Sendang Gile and Tiu Kelep).

Day 01:

After Breakfast at 07.00am, transfer to Sembalun village (1.5 hour drive). Register at the Rinjani Information Center (RIC) in Sembalun Lawang (1,156 m), meet your guide and arrange tickets. The trek starts with gentle ascent walking through open grassland. Rest at Pos 1 (1,300m), Pos 2 (1,500 m) and Pos 3 (1,800 m). We ascend to Sembalun Crater Rim (2,639m). Set up camp for the first night and enjoy the spectacular sunset.

Day 02:

Wake up at 02:00 am with hot drinks and light breakfast. Climb to the summit (3,726m) taking around 3 hours. Enjoy the breathtaking sunrise. Descend back to camp for breakfast. After resting, we descend to Segara Anak Lake (2,000m) which takes about 3 hours. Camp the second night by the lake. Visit the natural hot springs just a 10-minute walk from the camp to soothe your aching muscles.

Day 03:

Enjoy a relaxing morning at the lake. After lunch, leave the lake and climb for 2.5 hours up to Senaru Crater Rim (2,641m). Camp the third night at Senaru Crater Rim. Enjoy the incredible sunset over Mount Agung in Bali and the smoke coming out of the new volcano hole.

Day 04:

After breakfast and a beautiful sunrise, trek down to Senaru village through the tropical forest. You might see the rare black monkey. The descent takes about 5 hours. The trek ends at Rinjani Trek Center in Senaru around 13:30 (Rinjani Excellence Office). Transfer out to your next destination.
