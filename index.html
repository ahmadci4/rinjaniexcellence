      Torean Route – The Most Scenic Trail

      The Torean route offers a completely different trekking experience compared to other routes. Starting from Torean Village (~600 m), the trail leads to Segara Anak Lake (~2,00 m) through a unique landscape.

      Unlike other routes, Torean features:

      • River-side trekking
      • Natural waterfalls
      • Lush green valleys
      • Less crowded atmosphere

      One of the highlights is Birisan Nangka, a scenic point with dramatic cliffs and valley views. This rute is highly recommended as a descent option for those who want a more natural and scenic experience.

      How to Choose the Right Route ?

      Each trekking route has its own advantages. You should choose based on:

      • Your fitness level
      • Trekking experience
      • Your goal (summit, lake, or relaxation)

      Mount Rinjani Trekking Programs

      Official trekking durations range from 2 Days 1 Night to 4 Days 3 Nights.

      🔹 2 Days 1 Night – Senaru Crater Rim: Suitable for beginners and families. Mostly through tropical forest and less physically demanding.

      🔹 2 Days 1 Night – Sembalun Summit: Best for those aiming to reach the summit in a short time. Requires good physical fitness.

      🔹 3 Days 2 Nights – Summit, Lake & Hot Spring: Designed for experienced trekkers. Not recommended for beginners due to long trekking hours (up to 10 hours on Day 2).

      🔹 4 Days 3 Nights – Relax Trekking Program: A more relaxed itinerary with shorter daily trekking duration (~8 hours). Ideal for those who want to enjoy the journey without rushing.
