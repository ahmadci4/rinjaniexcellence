      Torean Route – The Most Scenic Trail

      The Torean route offers a completely different trekking experience compared to other routes. Starting from Torean Village (~600 m), the trail leads to Segara Anak Lake (~2,000 m) through a unique landscape.

      Unlike other routes, Torean features:

      • River-side trekking
      • Natural waterfalls
      • Lush green valleys
      • Less crowded atmosphere

      One of the highlights is Birisan Nangka, a scenic point with dramatic cliffs and valley views. This route is highly recommended as a descent option for those who want a more natural and scenic experience.

      How to Choose the Right Route?

      Each trekking route has its own advantages. You should choose based on:

      • Your fitness level
      • Trekking experience
      • Your goal (summit, lake, or relaxation)

      Mount Rinjani Trekking Programs

      Official trekking durations range from 2 Days 1 Night to 4 Days 3 Nights.

      🔹 2 Days 1 Night – Senaru Crater Rim

      Suitable for beginners and families. Mostly through tropical forest and less physically demanding.

      🔹 2 Days 1 Night – Sembalun Summit

      Best for those aiming to reach the summit in a short time. Requires good physical fitness.

      🔹 3 Days 2 Nights – Summit, Lake & Hot Spring

      Designed for experienced trekkers. Not recommended for beginners due to long trekking hours.

      🔹 4 Days 3 Nights – Relax Trekking Program

      A more relaxed itinerary with shorter daily trekking duration (~8 hours). Ideal for enjoying the journey.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      When is the best time to hike Mount Rinjani?
      The best time to hike is during the dry season, from April to November. The national park is officially closed from January to March due to heavy rain.
      Do I need to carry my own tent and food?
      No! Our dedicated porters will carry all camping equipment, sleeping bags, and food. You only need to carry a small daypack.
