      Torean Route – The Most Scenic Trail

      The Torean route offers a completely different trekking experience compared to other routes. Starting from Torean Village (~600 m), the trail leads to Segara Anak Lake (~2,000 m) through a unique landscape.

      Unlike other routes, Torean features:

      • River-side trekking
      • Natural waterfalls
      • Lush green valleys
      • Less crowded atmosphere

      One of the highlights is Birisan Nangka, a scenic point with dramatic cliffs and valley views. Highly recommended as a descent option for those who want a more natural experience.
