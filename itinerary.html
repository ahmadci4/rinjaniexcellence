Complete Trekking Itineraries

Review our detailed plans and prepare for the journey of a lifetime.

Package 1

2 Days 1 Night Summit

Start from Sembalun to Sembalun Crater Rim, camp, climb summit, and return to Sembalun.

Day 0: Arrival

Pick up at Lombok area, transfer to Senaru/Sembalun hotel. Briefing with guide and warming up walk to Sindang Gile waterfall.

Day 1: Sembalun to Crater Rim (2639m)

Start 8:00 AM from Sembalun. Trek through open meadow. Pass Pos 1, 2, and 3. Steep climb to crater rim. Camp and enjoy sunset.

Day 2: Summit (3726m) & Descent

Wake up 2:00 AM. Trek to summit for sunrise. Descend to camp for breakfast, then trek down to Sembalun (5 hours). Transfer to next destination.

Package 2

3 Days 2 Nights: Summit, Lake & Hot Spring

Sembalun Crater Rim - Summit, Lake & Hot Spring - Senaru Crater Rim

Day 1: Sembalun to Crater Rim (2639m)

Transfer to Sembalun. Trek through open grassland. Rest at Pos 1, 2, and 3. Ascend to Sembalun Crater Rim. Camp overnight.

Day 2: Summit, Lake & Hot Springs

Wake 2:00 AM for Summit (3726m). Return for breakfast. Descend to Segara Anak Lake (2000m) for lunch and hot springs. Climb to Senaru Crater Rim (2641m) for 2nd night camp.

Day 3: Descent to Senaru

Trek down through thick tropical forest (4-5 hours) to Senaru village. Transfer to next destination in Lombok.

Package 3

4 Days 3 Nights: Complete Explorer

A more relaxed pace to enjoy all of Mount Rinjani's offerings.

Day 1: Ascent to Sembalun Crater Rim

Trek from Sembalun Lawang. Pass POS 1, 2, 3. Steep climb to Sembalun Crater Rim. Set up camp.

Day 2: Summit & Lake Camping

Summit attack at 2:00 AM. After breakfast, descend to Segara Anak Lake. Camp by the lake and visit Hot Springs.

Day 3: Relax at Lake & Climb to Senaru Rim

Relaxing morning. After lunch, climb 2.5 hours to Senaru Crater Rim. Camp here with views of Mt. Agung, Bali.

Day 4: Jungle Descent to Senaru

Trek down 5 hours through tropical forest. Trek ends at Rinjani Excellence Office around 13:30.
