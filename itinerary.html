      Package 1

      Mount Rinjani Trekking 2 DAYS 1 Night Via senaru village

      This route appeals to those short of time , for climbing to Mt. Rinjani’s Crater Rim in Gunung Rinjani National Park. Starting and ending in Senaru village , it provides spectacular views of Lake Segara Anak within the new volcano’s crater.

      This gentle trek is good for beginners, or families with children ( minimum 6 years old ) Tour can leave every day except weather condition does not permitted.

      Highlights Are: Spectacular Sunset and Sunrise at the Senaru Crater Rim

      TREK ITINERARY:

      Day 0 :

      Pick up service ( anywhere around of Lombok ) then transfer to Senaru Village and Check in at senaru accommodation / Lodge ( with hot/cold water, air condition ), briefing and free at your leisure. If time allows ( 3 hours before sunset ) you can visit the Famous Waterfall in Senaru ( Sendang Gile and Tiu Kelep Waterfall & Senaru traditional village .

      Day 1 :

      Breakfast at 06,30 and Register at the Rinjani Trek Centre (RTC) in Senaru (601m) early in the morning you meet your guide and he arranges the National Park entry or checking barcode Code ticket E-Rinjani . The RTC staff briefing describes the route details, local cultural traditions and what you might see, and explains rubbish management and safety procedures.

      The first hours of the trek climb steeply through thick tropical forest rich in flora, bird life and variety of butterflies. You might see orchid and if lucky, the rare black ebony leaf monkey, known locally as lutung. Rest stops are taken along the way, and at pos 2 Montong Satas shelter ( 1,500m ). Village stories and local legends are related by your guide. Features include the Bayan tree bunut Ngenkang that looks like someone standing with their legs apart, and the batu penyesalan stone.

      Depending on your preference and weather conditions, the night’s camp is made either in the forest at pos 3 Mondokon Lolak (2,000m) after about five hours climb from senaru, or continue the ascent for another couple of hours through grassy meadows Plawangan 1 Senaru crater rim at 2,641m. from the crater rim are sweeping views across the lake Segara Anak (2,00m) to the summit of Mt. Rinjani. Within the crater, Mt. Baru ( 2,351m) is active, last erupting dramatically in 2009 On a clear evening the Gili Isles, Bali and Mt. Agung can be seen to the west.

      Day 2 :

      Sunrise at the crater rim is memorable to Day 2, enjoyed after a pre-dawn two hour climb for those camping at pos 3 or we called Mondokon Lolak. After enjoying the view, descend down the trail down to Senaru village through first grassland then back into the shade of the tropical forest.

      After about seven hours from the rim, the trek ends at the road head in Senaru . Trek ends in ( CV. RINJANI EXCELLENT OFFICE ) it is around 13,30pm and then Transfer out to your next destination ( Following the spot on Day 0 ).

      Package 2

      2 Days 1 Night Sembalun Summit

      Paket pendakian ini dirancang khusus bagi pendaki yang memiliki stamina fisik sangat baik dan ingin mencapai puncak dalam waktu singkat. Jalur Sembalun memungkinkan Anda untuk mencapai puncak tertinggi Rinjani (3.726 mdpl) hanya dalam 2 hari 1 malam.

      Itinerary Overview:

      • Day 1: Sembalun Village - Sembalun Crater Rim Mulai pendakian dari desa Sembalun (1.100m) melewati padang savana yang luas. Menginap di Sembalun Crater Rim (2.639m) untuk beristirahat sebelum penyerangan puncak (Summit Attack).
      • Day 2: Summit Rinjani - Sembalun Village Bangun pukul 02:00 pagi, mulai mendaki medan berpasir yang curam menuju Puncak Rinjani (3.726m). Menikmati sunrise spektakuler, lalu turun kembali ke Crater Rim untuk sarapan, dan lanjut turun ke Sembalun Village.
      Package 3

      3 Days 2 Nights Summit & Lake

      Designed for experienced trekkers. Not recommended for beginners due to long trekking hours (up to 10 hours on Day 2). Ini adalah paket trekking Rinjani yang paling diminati yang mencakup semua destinasi utama.

      Itinerary Overview:

      • Day 1: Sembalun - Sembalun Crater Rim Mulai perjalanan dari Sembalun Gate. Berjalan di tengah padang savana menuju Pos 1, 2, dan 3. Dilanjutkan dengan tanjakan terjal menuju Crater Rim Sembalun. Menginap malam pertama di sini.
      • Day 2: Summit - Segara Anak Lake - Senaru Crater Rim Summit attack jam 2 pagi. Setelah sarapan di camp, perjalanan dilanjutkan menuruni tebing ke Danau Segara Anak. Waktu bebas untuk mandi di air panas alami. Setelah makan siang, mendaki lagi menuju Senaru Crater Rim untuk bermalam.
      • Day 3: Senaru Crater Rim - Senaru Village Turun melintasi hutan hujan lebat menuju Desa Senaru. Diperkirakan tiba pada siang/sore hari, lalu diantar menuju destinasi Anda selanjutnya di Lombok.
      Package 4

      4 Days 3 Nights Relax Trekking

      A more relaxed itinerary with shorter daily trekking duration (~8 hours). Ideal for those who want to enjoy the journey without rushing. Cocok untuk menikmati alam Gunung Rinjani dengan ritme santai.

      Itinerary Overview:

      • Day 1: Sembalun - Sembalun Crater Rim Trekking santai melewati savana menuju Crater Rim Sembalun.
      • Day 2: Summit - Segara Anak Lake Penyerangan puncak, lalu turun santai menuju Danau Segara Anak dan bermalam di tepi danau yang sejuk.
      • Day 3: Segara Anak Lake - Senaru Crater Rim Pagi hari dihabiskan sepenuhnya untuk relaksasi di area danau dan air panas. Siang hari mendaki perlahan menuju Senaru Crater Rim.
      • Day 4: Senaru Crater Rim - Senaru Village Menuruni hutan tropis Rinjani dengan udara yang sejuk hingga tiba kembali di basecamp Senaru.
      Package 5

      Torean Route - The Scenic Trail

      The Torean route offers a completely different trekking experience compared to other routes. Starting from Torean Village (~600 m), the trail leads to Segara Anak Lake (~2,000 m) through a unique landscape.

      Unlike other routes, Torean features: River-side trekking, Natural waterfalls, Lush green valleys, and a Less crowded atmosphere. One of the highlights is Birisan Nangka, a scenic point with dramatic cliffs and valley views. This route is highly recommended as a descent option for those who want a more natural and scenic experience.

      Itinerary Overview (3D2N Sembalun - Torean):

      • Day 1: Sembalun - Sembalun Crater Rim Melewati rute savana klasik menuju tempat peristirahatan pertama di bibir kawah Sembalun.
      • Day 2: Summit Rinjani - Danau Segara Anak Menuju puncak saat subuh. Kembali ke tenda, lalu turun ke area Danau Segara Anak untuk bersantai dan bermalam.
      • Day 3: Segara Anak Lake - Torean Village Hari paling magis! Pulang melalui lembah Torean yang mengikuti aliran sungai dari danau, melewati tebing raksasa dan air terjun menawan hingga tiba di Desa Torean.
