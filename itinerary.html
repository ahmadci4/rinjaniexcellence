Package 1

MOUNT RINJANI TREKING 2 DAYS 1 NIGHT SUMMIT

Mount Rinjani 2 days 1 night trek start from Sembalun to the Sembalun Crater rim, camping tent, climb to summit Rinjani and return to Sembalun village in East Lombok.

This Hikking Mount Rinjani program the signt for climbers or trekker in good level of fitness and able to walk up and down for an average of 8.5 hours per day.

Mount Rinjani Trekking Summit 2 Days 1 Night.
(Sembalun Village - Sembalun Crater Rim - Summit)

Arrival Day On Lombok / Day 0:

We'll doing pick you up at the Lombok or other place on Lombok island and transfer to the hotel in Senaru or Sembalun village (on request).

Meet Your trekking guide will provide guidance to better prepare you for the journey and we will guide you to Sindang Gile waterfall for warming up before trekking Rinjani next day and back to hotel provided then dinner.

Day 1:

Wake up at 06:30 and get breakfast. After that, 07:00 we will drive you to the village of Sembalun (1100 m) and register at the Rinjani Information Center Office Sembalun

Trekking start from the village of Sembalun around 8:00 am. We start with a gentle climb through open meadow. After about 2 hours of walking, we will reach the post 1 (1300m), and another 40 minutes to Pos 2 (1500m). We will continue to Pos 3 (1800m) and have lunch here.

After lunch and rest, we will go to Sembalun crater rim (2639m). It takes 3 hours including a prolonged steep climb. We will camp the night on the crater rim. We can enjoy the view of sunrise and sunset.

Day 2:

We wake up around 02:00 am. under the cover of darkness and our guide and porter serve you with a hot drink and some toast with hot drink coffe and tea for light breakfast. We will start heading to the summit of Rinjani at 03:00 am. We start with a moderate trek, followed by a fairly easy but longer trek. Final approach that involves climbing up steep volcanic screen loser.

At 6:00.am We can reach the summit (3726 m) with a beautiful sunrise, and panoramic view of Lombok island, the island of Bali, the island of Sumbawa and Segara Anak Lake.

After that, we will descend into the crater rim Sembalun within 2 hours and have breakfast here. Then will continue to go down to the village of Sembalun, which takes 5 hours to reach down. Car will take us to the village of Senaru (Rinjani Excellent) and we'll transfer to your the next destination.

The First Day Doing Hiking Trek Itinerary.

Day: 01

Our guide and driver meet you at Senaru accommodation and will be pick up you at lobby at 06.00 am. Then transfer to Sembalun Village by the car. And register / check in barcode code of the ticket and check up at office Rinjani Information Center (RIC) in Sembalun Lawang (1,156 m), and your guide and arrange National Park entry tickets. Your guide gives you a briefing describing fews minute the route details, local cultural traditions and what do you might see thing to do and explains rubbish management and safety procedures.

The trek starts from Sembalun Lawang with the gentle ascent walking through open grassland used as cattle grazing by local people. Resting at Pos 1 Pemantauan (1,300), after about three hours from Sembalun we reach Pos 2 Tengengean (1,500 m) enjoying the beautiful views of Mt. Rinjani summit (3,726 m). After another hours ascent to Pos 3 Pada Balong (1,800 m), we start the step 3,5 climb amidst the pine-like Casuarinas species locally known as Cemara, perhaps seeing evidence of wild pigs and the long-tailed grey macaque monkeys to the camp site at Plawangan 2 Sembalun crater rim (2,639 m).

Day: 02

Wake up at 02:00 am early in the morning and have a light breakfast. The hardest part of the trek is on the 3rd day, before heading to the summit of Mt Rinjani (3,726 m). Start ascending at 02:30am. It takes about three hours. The first part is step dirt track about 1 hours, the second part is long trek and slightly up for about 1.5 hours, the last about 1 hour is steep and loosely ground (sands and stones). Once you get there, you'll be feeling proud on top of Mt Rinjani.

You can enjoy incredible scenery such as: Sunrise and panoramic view of Lombok island, the island of Bali, the island of Sumbawa and Segara Anak Lake.

After sunrise, we have to go down back to Sembalun crater rim and get a hot breakfast. After breakfast and having a rest, we'll continue our trek down to Segara anak lake. The trail down is steep and rocky. It takes 2.5 until 3 hours to get there.

We'll set up camp by the lake and rest. Hot spring just 10 minutes walking from lake, where you can soak in healing your skin and get your muscle relax. We'll spend the night here and enjoy the beautiful scenery of the lake Segara anak and Mt Barujari

Day: 03

Breakfast by the lake Segara anak and then we will be going down to Senaru village, it take about 6 hours. After 2 hours walking up from the lake we will reach Senaru crater rim (2,641 m). We can enjoy incredible scenery of the lake, Mt Barujari and the summit from this point. Then continue going down to pos 3 Mondokon lolak (2,000m) and we will get lunch here.

After Lunch and rest, we'll go down to Senaru village again, walking through tropical forest. If you are lucky, you'll see the black monkey. It takes 4 hours until Senaru village and trekking will finish here around 03:00pm.

When you arrived in Senaru village then transfer you back to your next destination. And the tours finish.

Package 3

Mount Rinjani Trekking 4 Days 3 Night (Sembalun Crater Rim - Summit Lake & Hot spring - Senaru Crater Rim)

MOUNT RINJANI TREK ITINERARY:

Day 0:

Pick up service (Lombok Airport, Lembar Harbor, Mataram City, Senggigi Beach, Teluk Nare, Teluk Kodeq, Bangsal Harbor, Kayangan Harbor, Tete Batu) then transfer to Senaru Village and Check in at Senaru Accommodation (with hot & cold water, ), briefing and free at your leisure. If time allowing (3 hours before sunset) you can visit the Famous Waterfall in Lombok (Sendang Gile and Tiu Kelep Waterfall.

Day 01:

After Breakfast at 07.00am and transfer to sembalun village, drive for 1.5 hour, Register the Rinjani Information Center (RIC) in Sembalun Lawang (1,156 m), meet your guide and arrange National Park entry tickets and with medical chek. Your guide gives you a briefing describing the route details, local cultural traditions and what you might see, and explains rubbish management and safety procedures. The trek starts from Sembalun Lawang with the gentle ascent walking through open grassland used as cattle grazing by local people. Resting at Pos 1 Pemantauan (1,300), after about three hours from Sembalun we reach Pos 2 Tengengean (1,500 m) enjoying the beautiful views of Mt. Rinjani summit (3,726 m). After another hours ascent to Pos 3 Pada Balong (1,800 m), we start the step 3,5 climb amidst the pine-like Casuarinas species locally known as Cemara, perhaps seeing evidence of wild pigs and the long-tailed gray macaque monkeys to the campsite at Plawangan II Sembalun crater rim (2,639 m).

Day 02:

At 03:00 AM starts are necessary for the hard three-hours climb to the summit (3,726 m) on volcanic screen. Once there, enjoy proud feelings of satisfaction and the amazing sunrise views across the Wallace Line to Bali, Mt. Agung and Sumbawa, after breakfast down to the lake Segara Anak (2,000 m) within the Rinjani crater. Mt. Baru is an island in the lake and an active volcano, last erupting dramatically in 1994. Return down the ridge passing numerous edelweiss plants Bunga Abadi to the crater rim camp for a well-deserved breakfast. It takes two to three hours down the steep trail to the crater lake and day 2's camp. The rest of the day is spent relaxing, swimming in the lake and exploring the caves and pilgrimage hot springs, believed to have healing powers.

Day 03:

After breakfast, leave the lake and climb for two and half hours up to Plawangan 1 senaru Crater Rim (2,461 m). After lunch enjoy the views of the lake from the other side, relax, explore and await the sunset. If the wind is cold, you may prefer to descend one hour to Cemara Lima and camp in the more sheltered forest.

Day 04:

After breakfast, descend down the long trail to Senaru village (601 m), through thick tropical forests rich in flora, bird life and a variety of butterflies. You might see orchid and, if lucky, the rare black ebony leaf monkey, known locally as lutung. Rest stop are taken along the way, with village stories and local legends related by your guide. Features include the Batu Penyesalan stone and banyan tree bunut ngengkang that looks like someone standing with their legs apart. After seven hours walk from the rim, (or five hours from Pos 3 Mondokon Lolak), the trek ends at Rinjani Trek Center at the road head in Senaru, Trek end it a round 13:30, (Rinjani Excellent Office) and then Transfer out to your next destination (following tho Spot on Day 0)

Mount Rinjani Trekking 2 Days 1 Night Via Senaru Village

This route appeals to those short of time, for climbing to Mt. Rinjani's Crater Rim in Gunung Rinjani National Park. Starting and ending in Senaru village, it provides spectacular views of Lake Segara Anak within the new volcano's crater. This gentle trek is good for beginners, or families with children (minimum 6 years old).

TREK ITINERARY:

Day 0:

Pick up service (anywhere around of Lombok) then transfer to Senaru Village and Check in at senaru accommodation / Lodge (with hot/cold water, air condition), briefing and free at your leisure. If time allows (3 hours before sunset) you can visit the Famous Waterfall in Senaru (Sendang Gile and Tiu Kelep Waterfall & Senaru traditional village.

Day 1:

Breakfast at 06,30 and Register at the Rinjani Trek Centre (RTC) in Senaru (601m) early in the morning you meet your guide and he arranges the National Park entry or checking barcode Code ticket E-Rinjani. The RTC staff briefing describes the route details, local cultural traditions and what you might see, and explains rubbish management and safety procedures. The first hours of the trek climb steeply through thick tropical forest rich in flora, bird life and variety of butterflies. You might see orchid and if lucky, the rare black ebony leaf monkey.
